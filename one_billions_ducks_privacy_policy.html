Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Game in Budget handles information in One Billions Ducks: Hunting Simulator (the “Game”), including advertising and in-app purchases.

1. Overview

Game in Budget publishes One Billions Ducks: Hunting Simulator on Android. The Game may use third-party services to display advertisements and process optional in-app purchases. Those services may collect and process information under their own privacy policies.

This policy applies to the Game and does not cover other apps or websites that you may visit.

2. Information that may be collected

Depending on the Game features, your device settings, and the third-party services used, information processed may include:

  • Device and app information: device type, operating system, app version, language, and technical identifiers.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers (where available), ad views, clicks, and information used to deliver, measure, or limit ads.
  • Diagnostics: crash, error, performance, and fraud-prevention signals processed by service providers.
  • Purchase information: product identifiers, purchase status, and transaction tokens or receipts required to deliver or restore purchases. We do not receive your full payment card number from Google Play.
  • Game progress: settings, progress, and unlock status that the Game may store on your device.

We do not ask you to create a Game in Budget account to use the Game. If you email us, we receive the contact details and message you choose to send.

3. Advertising — Unity Ads

The Game uses Unity Ads to display advertising, which may include rewarded ads and other ad formats. Unity and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location inferred from network information, ad interaction data, and other technical information to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements.

Where required, the Game or the ad provider should request consent or offer applicable privacy choices before processing information for personalized advertising.

Learn more: Unity Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases — Google Play

The Game offers optional digital purchases, such as permanent in-game upgrades. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing, and payment details are handled by Google rather than by Game in Budget.

To provide purchased content and help restore entitlements, the Game may process purchase status, product IDs, and transaction information provided by Google Play. Purchases are subject to Google Play's applicable terms and policies.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.

5. Game progress and local storage

The Game may save gameplay progress, preferences, and unlock information locally on your device. Removing the Game or clearing app storage may delete locally stored progress. Some purchased items may be restorable through the Google Play account used to buy them.

6. Sharing, purpose, and retention

Information may be processed by service providers such as Unity (advertising) and Google (billing and related platform services) to operate the Game, provide purchases, serve and measure ads, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell players' contact information directly. Third-party advertising services may handle data in ways described in their own policies, including disclosures to advertising partners.

Information held by third parties is retained according to their policies. Locally saved game data generally remains until you clear app data or uninstall the Game. Support emails may be kept as reasonably necessary to respond to requests and meet legal obligations.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

  • You can manage Android advertising privacy settings, including resetting or deleting your advertising ID where supported.
  • You can decline optional rewarded ads; doing so may mean you do not receive the associated in-game reward.
  • You can manage purchase history and payment settings through Google Play.
  • You can clear locally stored Game data through Android app settings or uninstall the Game.
  • You can contact us to ask questions or request help with personal information we control. For information held by Unity or Google, their own privacy tools and policies may also apply.

Privacy rights differ by location, and we will review applicable requests in accordance with relevant law.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is not intended to collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and purchase features may involve third-party data processing. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the Game and purchases.

Publisher review required: If the Game targets children or includes children in its target audience, additional Google Play Families requirements and child-appropriate ad configuration may apply. This policy alone does not establish compliance.

9. Data security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards for information we control. However, no internet transmission, device storage, or third-party service is guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Game features, service providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. Continued use of the Game after an update is subject to applicable law.
